## Strategic Shift: Partnership-First GTM

### Previous GTM Strategy (Solo)
- Target facilities directly with PitchTracker standalone product
- Compete with consumer radar guns (TAG Sports, Pocket Radar) on price/features
- Build consumer features (mobile app, portability) to expand market
- **Problem:** Setup complexity prevents consumer adoption; facility market is slow to penetrate

### New GTM Strategy (Partnership)
- **Partner with TAG Sports** to create integrated ecosystem
- TAG Sports owns consumer market ($230 radar), PitchTracker owns facility market ($1,200 stereo)
- Athletes use TAG at home → join PitchTracker facilities → data flows seamlessly
- **Advantage:** Leverage TAG's 10K+ users as qualified lead pipeline; differentiate vs. Rapsodo/TrackMan

**This is a strategic accelerator, not a pivot.** Core product remains unchanged. Distribution and positioning are enhanced through partnership.

## Market Segmentation (Clarified by TAG Sports)

### Consumer Market (TAG Sports Serves)
**Customer:** Individual athletes, parents, youth/HS players
**Need:** Affordable at-home practice tracking
**Budget:** $200-400 (one-time purchase)
**Setup:** <5 minutes (portable, smartphone)
**Metrics:** Speed only (sufficient for practice)
**Platform:** Mobile app (iOS/Android)
**Volume:** High (10,000s of individual users)

**TAG Sports Positioning:** "Track your pitches at home for $230. No subscription. No hassle."

---

### Facility Market (PitchTracker Serves)
**Customer:** Training facilities, academies, high schools
**Need:** Professional 3D pitch tracking for serious development
**Budget:** $1,000-2,000 (amortized across many athletes)
**Setup:** 30-60 minutes (fixed installation, acceptable for facilities)
**Metrics:** 3D trajectory, movement, location, strike zone (professional insights)
**Platform:** Desktop app (Windows)
**Volume:** Low (100s of facilities, but higher revenue per customer)

**PitchTracker Positioning:** "Professional 3D pitch tracking for training facilities - Rapsodo-level insights at 1/5th the cost. Integrates with TAG Sports."

---

### Integrated Ecosystem (Partnership Creates)

**Customer Journey:**
1. **Discovery (Consumer):** Athlete discovers pitch tracking via TAG Sports (Instagram, retail, word-of-mouth)
2. **Practice (Consumer):** Athlete uses TAG Sports at home ($230 investment)
3. **Commitment (Facility):** Athlete seeks professional training, searches for facilities
4. **Enrollment (Facility):** Athlete enrolls in PitchTracker-equipped facility ($75/month)
5. **Continuity (Integrated):** TAG Sports practice data flows into PitchTracker facility sessions
6. **Retention (Ecosystem):** Athlete invested in both platforms (high switching costs)

**Lifetime Value:**
- TAG Sports alone: $230 (one-time)
- TAG Sports + PitchTracker facility: $230 + ($75/month × 12-24 months) = $1,130-2,030
- **Partnership unlocks 4-9× LTV increase**

## Revenue Model (Revised)

### PitchTracker Revenue Streams

#### Stream 1: Facility System Sales (Core - Unchanged)
**Model:** Sell PitchTracker system to facilities (one-time purchase + optional support)
**Price:** $1,200 (standard) or $2,000 (with hardware bundle)
**Target:** 50-100 facilities in Year 1
**Revenue:** $60,000-200,000 (Year 1)

#### Stream 2: TAG Sports Referrals (NEW - Partnership)
**Model:** Facilities pay $75-100/month per athlete. TAG Sports refers athletes to PitchTracker facilities.
**Mechanism:** TAG users search "Find PitchTracker Facilities Near Me" → enroll → referral tracked
**Revenue Share:** PitchTracker keeps 85-90%, TAG Sports earns 10-15% referral fee
**Projected:** 1,000 TAG users → facilities = $900K annual facility revenue → PitchTracker earns $765K-810K

#### Stream 3: Hardware Bundles (NEW - Partnership)
**Model:** Sell TAG Sports devices to facilities (equip athletes for home practice)
**Bundle:** PitchTracker ($1,200) + 10× TAG devices ($2,300) = $3,000 (save $500)
**Revenue Split:** TAG earns hardware ($2,300), PitchTracker earns software ($700)
**Projected:** 20 bundles in Year 1 = $14,000 revenue to PitchTracker

#### Stream 4: Support & Training (Future)
**Model:** Offer premium support plans to high-volume facilities
**Price:** $200-500/year (priority support, onboarding assistance, custom training)
**Projected:** 20% attach rate on facility sales = $2,400-10,000/year

**Total Year 1 Revenue (with TAG Partnership):**
- Facility system sales: $60,000-200,000
- TAG referral-driven revenue: $700,000-800,000
- Hardware bundles: $14,000
- **Total: $774,000-1,014,000** (vs. $60K-200K solo)

**Partnership multiplier: 4-13× revenue increase**

## Risk Mitigation (Partnership-Specific)

### Risk 1: TAG Sports Doesn't Respond to Partnership Outreach
**Mitigation:**
- Warm introduction (research mutual contacts, investors)
- Strong initial value proposition (revenue model, competitive moat)
- Low-risk pilot offer (2-4 weeks development, 90-day test)
- **Backup:** Approach other consumer radar companies (Pocket Radar, Bushnell)

### Risk 2: TAG Sports Demands Unfavorable Terms
**Mitigation:**
- Walk-away point defined (max 20% referral fee, 2-year exclusivity only)
- Alternative partners identified (plan B)
- Solo GTM remains viable (partnership is accelerator, not requirement)

### Risk 3: Integration Doesn't Drive Adoption
**Mitigation:**
- Pilot first (validate demand before full buildout)
- Athlete incentives ($50 credit for using integration)
- Simplify workflow (one-click export/import)
- Marketing education (show value of data continuity)
