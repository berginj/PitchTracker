## Program Overview

The PitchTracker Pilot Program is a **structured 90-day evaluation** with 2-3 select baseball/softball facilities to validate product-market fit, measure accuracy, and refine the product before broader commercial release.

**Original Program Window:** April 2026 - June 2026 (flexible start dates per partner)

**Status Note (2026-06-22):** Pilot launch is pending camera alignment and
validation. Treat the dates below as the original plan until the owner records
actual deployment status.

### What This Is
- **Structured validation program** with clear success metrics
- **Partnership** - we work with you to ensure success
- **Feedback-driven** - your input shapes the product roadmap
- **No cost to pilot partners** during pilot period

### What This Is Not
- Open-ended beta testing or bug hunting
- Free trial with no commitment (we need active usage and feedback)
- Support-light early access (we provide hands-on support)

## Ideal Pilot Partner Profile

### Required Characteristics
✅ **Facility Type:** Baseball/softball academy, training facility, or high school program
✅ **Session Volume:** Run 10+ pitching sessions per week (or commit to pilot usage)
✅ **Technical Capability:** Has operator comfortable with laptop setup and calibration
✅ **Physical Setup:** Can accommodate dual camera setup (behind plate, 8-12 feet apart)
✅ **Commitment:** Willing to provide weekly feedback and participate in check-ins
✅ **Data Sharing:** Willing to share anonymized usage data for validation

### Ideal Characteristics (Not Required)
⭐ Already uses competing product (Rapsodo, Pocket Radar) for comparison
⭐ Serves multiple pitchers with varying skill levels (data diversity)
⭐ Has access to reference equipment (radar gun, Stalker, TrackMan)
⭐ Open to being public reference customer post-pilot
⭐ Located within driving distance for on-site support

### Not a Good Fit
❌ Individual pitchers or parents (setup complexity too high)
❌ Facilities with no dedicated operator (requires consistent setup)
❌ Programs running <5 sessions per week (insufficient usage data)
❌ Facilities unwilling to share usage feedback

## Success Metrics

### What "Pilot Success" Means

Success is measured across 4 dimensions:

#### 1. Usage Adoption (CRITICAL)
**Target:** 20+ sessions in 90 days per facility
- **Excellent:** 40+ sessions (2-3 per week)
- **Good:** 20-40 sessions (1-2 per week)
- **Acceptable:** 15-20 sessions
- **Failed:** <15 sessions

**Measurement:** Session count from PitchTracker logs

#### 2. Accuracy Validation (CRITICAL)
**Target:** Velocity measurements within reference-equipment bounds established by the validation protocol
- **Excellent:** ≤1 mph MAE (mean absolute error)
- **Good:** ≤1.5 mph MAE
- **Acceptable:** ≤2 mph MAE
- **Failed:** >2.5 mph MAE

**Measurement:** Side-by-side comparison with Pocket Radar/Stalker/Rapsodo

#### 3. Workflow Adoption (HIGH PRIORITY)
**Target:** Operators use PitchTracker without support after week 2
- **Excellent:** Self-service from week 1, no support calls after initial setup
- **Good:** 1-2 support calls in first 2 weeks, then self-sufficient
- **Acceptable:** Occasional support calls, no recurring issues
- **Failed:** Frequent support calls, operators struggling with workflow

**Measurement:** Support ticket count, weekly feedback calls

#### 4. User Satisfaction (HIGH PRIORITY)
**Target:** Operators and coaches find value in insights provided
- **Excellent:** "This changed how we coach" - proactive feature requests
- **Good:** "Very useful, would pay for this" - positive feedback
- **Acceptable:** "Helpful, some issues but workable" - neutral feedback
- **Failed:** "Frustrated, not worth the setup time" - negative feedback

**Measurement:** Qualitative feedback, Net Promoter Score survey

### Pilot Scorecard Template

Each facility gets scored on these metrics:

| Metric | Weight | Target | Actual | Score |

**Scoring:**
- **8-10:** Pilot success - proceed to commercial relationship
- **6-7.9:** Mixed results - evaluate friction points, potential retry
- **<6:** Pilot failure - product not ready or partner not ideal fit

## Pilot Timeline

### Week 0: Pre-Pilot Setup
- **Partner Agreement Signed** - MOU or simple agreement
- **Hardware Shipped** (if not already owned) - Cameras, mounting hardware
- **Setup Scheduled** - On-site or remote setup assistance
- **Success Metrics Reviewed** - Ensure alignment on expectations

### Weeks 1-2: Installation & Calibration
- **Initial Setup** - Install cameras, calibrate with ChArUco board
- **ROI Configuration** - Set up lane and strike zone
- **Training Session** - Train operator on session start/end workflow
- **First Test Sessions** - 2-3 supervised sessions to verify setup
- **Checkpoint:** Operator comfortable with basic workflow?

### Weeks 3-8: Active Usage
- **Regular Sessions** - Facility runs normal pitching sessions with PitchTracker
- **Weekly Check-Ins** - 15-minute call or email update
- **Issue Resolution** - Address bugs, calibration drift, workflow issues
- **Usage Tracking** - Monitor session count, detection rates
- **Checkpoint:** On track for 20+ sessions? Any recurring issues?

### Weeks 9-10: Accuracy Validation
- **Side-by-Side Testing** - Compare PitchTracker to reference equipment
- **Data Collection** - 100+ pitches with both systems running
- **Analysis** - Calculate MAE, detection rate, error bounds
- **Checkpoint:** Accuracy within the validation target? If not, diagnose issues

### Weeks 11-12: Wrap-Up & Evaluation
- **Final Feedback Session** - 30-60 minute structured interview
- **Scorecard Completion** - Review all success metrics
- **Pilot Report** - Document outcomes, lessons learned
- **Next Steps Decision** - Convert to commercial or close pilot
- **Checkpoint:** Overall pilot success? Proceed to commercial relationship?

### Post-Pilot: Transition
- **Commercial Agreement** (if successful) - Preferential pricing for year 1
- **Case Study** (if agreed) - Document facility's use case
- **Continued Support** - Move to standard support channel
- **Product Influence** - Priority feedback on roadmap items

## Pilot Evaluation Criteria

### When to Accept a Partner

**Green Light (Immediate Accept):**
- Runs 15+ pitching sessions per week
- Has dedicated operator who's tech-savvy
- Already uses competing product (Rapsodo/Pocket Radar)
- Located within driving distance for on-site support
- Enthusiastic about providing feedback

**Yellow Light (Discuss Further):**
- Runs 5-10 sessions per week (lower volume but acceptable)
- Operator is learning but motivated
- No competing product but open to validation testing
- Remote location (requires remote setup support)
- Interested but has questions/concerns

**Red Light (Decline or Defer):**
- Runs <5 sessions per week (insufficient usage data)
- No dedicated operator or operator is resistant
- Unrealistic expectations (wants consumer-simple setup immediately)
- Not willing to provide feedback or data sharing
- Expects free long-term access without commitment

### Partner Diversity Goals

Aim for diversity across:
- **Geography:** Not all in same region
- **Pitcher Level:** Youth, high school, college/adult
- **Facility Type:** Academy, school program, indoor facility
- **Velocity Range:** Mix of lower and higher velocity programs
- **Reference Equipment:** At least one with Rapsodo/Stalker for validation

## Risk Mitigation

### Risk: Partner Doesn't Use System
**Prevention:**
- Set clear usage expectations upfront (20+ sessions in 90 days)
- Weekly check-ins to maintain engagement
- Identify barriers early (setup issues, workflow friction)

**Response:**
- If <5 sessions by week 4, schedule intervention call
- Offer additional training or on-site visit
- If still no adoption by week 6, consider ending pilot early

### Risk: Accuracy Below Acceptable Threshold
**Prevention:**
- Ensure proper calibration during setup
- Verify detection parameters are optimized
- Test with reference equipment early (week 3-4)

**Response:**
- If MAE >2.5 mph, diagnose immediately (lighting, calibration, detection settings)
- Adjust parameters and re-test
- Document limitations if fundamental accuracy issue
- Be transparent with partner about findings

### Risk: Setup Complexity Overwhelms Operator
**Prevention:**
- Thorough initial training (2+ hours hands-on)
- Quick reference cards for common tasks
- Video walkthroughs for calibration steps

**Response:**
- Offer additional training sessions
- Create operator-specific cheat sheets
- Simplify workflow for that partner's specific use case
- Document friction points for product improvements

### Risk: Partner Expects Free Long-Term Access
**Prevention:**
- Clear communication upfront: "90-day pilot, then commercial decision"
- Pilot agreement states post-pilot pricing
- Regular reminders of pilot timeline

**Response:**
- Re-state pilot terms politely
- Offer preferential pricing (30% discount) as incentive
- If partner still resistant, gracefully end relationship

## Post-Pilot Decision Framework

### If Pilot Successful (Score ≥8/10)
1. **Propose Commercial Agreement**
   - 30% discount off standard pricing for year 1
   - Standard support tier
   - Continued product updates (all v1.x releases)

2. **Request Testimonial/Case Study**
   - Video testimonial (optional)
   - Written quote for marketing
   - Facility profile for case study (anonymized if preferred)

3. **Maintain Relationship**
   - Quarterly feedback sessions
   - Early access to major features
   - Priority support tier

### If Pilot Mixed Results (Score 6-7.9)
1. **Identify Specific Issues**
   - Was it accuracy, workflow, setup complexity, or feature gaps?
   - Can issues be resolved with product improvements?

2. **Offer Extended Pilot or Retry**
   - If issues being actively addressed: "Let's try another 30 days with improvements"
   - If partner still interested but cautious

3. **Part Ways Professionally**
   - Thank them for participation
   - Share validation results
   - Offer to reconnect when product matures

### If Pilot Failed (Score <6)
1. **Conduct Post-Mortem**
   - What went wrong? Product, partner fit, or external factors?
   - Learnings for future pilots

2. **Part Ways Professionally**
   - Thank them for honest feedback
   - Don't pressure for commercial relationship
   - Leave door open for future ("Check back with us in 6 months")

3. **Incorporate Learnings**
   - Update capability contract if systematic issues found
   - Adjust partner selection criteria
   - Improve product if fundamental gaps identified
