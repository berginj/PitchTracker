### Action 8: Negotiate and Sign MOU (Week 6-7) ⏰ 5-10 hours

**MOU Key Terms to Negotiate:**

1. **Referral Revenue Share**
   - PitchTracker proposal: TAG earns 10-15% of facility revenue from TAG-referred athletes
   - Negotiate: What's fair given TAG's contribution (lead gen) vs. PitchTracker's (platform)?
   - Acceptable range: 10-20% (prefer 10-15%)

2. **Exclusivity**
   - PitchTracker proposal: TAG is exclusive consumer radar partner for 2 years
   - TAG concern: "What if better opportunity comes?"
   - Compromise: 1-year exclusivity, renewable if partnership successful

3. **Pilot Structure**
   - Duration: 90 days
   - Facilities: 5-10 pilot partners
   - Success metrics: 50+ athletes, $1K+ referral revenue, NPS ≥8
   - Decision point: After 90 days, both parties decide to continue or end

4. **Engineering Investment**
   - Phase 1: Both parties commit 2-4 weeks engineering
   - Future phases: Contingent on Phase 1 success
   - Cost sharing: TAG covers mobile app, PitchTracker covers backend/cloud

5. **IP Ownership**
   - TAG Sports owns TAG app code and device firmware
   - PitchTracker owns PitchTracker app code and cloud platform
   - Shared: JSON schema and API spec (jointly owned)

6. **Data Ownership**
   - Athletes own their data (both parties are custodians)
   - Data portability: Athletes can export and delete
   - No exclusivity on data (athletes can share with others)

7. **Branding**
   - Co-branding on marketing materials
   - "Works with TAG Sports" logo for PitchTracker
   - "Integrates with PitchTracker" logo for TAG Sports app

8. **Exit Clause**
   - Either party can exit with 60 days notice
   - Wind-down period: 6 months (support existing integrated users)
   - Data export: All athletes get export of their data

**Legal Support:**
- Hire attorney to review MOU ($2,000-5,000)
- Ensure COPPA, GDPR, CCPA compliance
- Standard indemnification and liability clauses

**Deliverable:** MOU signed by both parties (non-binding but demonstrates commitment)
