# TAG Sports + PitchTracker Partnership Proposal

**One-Page Executive Summary for Initial Outreach**

---

## The Opportunity

Create an integrated **consumer-to-facility ecosystem** where TAG Sports owns at-home practice tracking and PitchTracker owns facility training analytics. Together, we turn individual athletes into facility customers and create a data network that benefits both companies.

---

## Why This Partnership Works

### Complementary Products (Not Competing)

| TAG Sports | PitchTracker |
|-----------|-------------|
| Consumer ($230 portable radar) | Facility ($1,200 stereo vision) |
| At-home practice tracking | Professional training analytics |
| Speed measurement | 3D trajectory + movement + location |
| Mobile app (iOS/Android) | Desktop app (Windows) |
| Individual athletes | Training facilities/academies |

**We serve different contexts in the same athlete's journey.**

## Revenue Model

### Stream 1: Facility Referrals
**TAG Sports earns 10-15% when TAG users enroll in PitchTracker facilities**

**Example:**
- 1,000 TAG users join facilities at $75/month
- $900,000 annual facility revenue
- **TAG Sports earns:** $90,000-135,000/year

**Sell TAG + PitchTracker as facility packages**

**Example:**

## Deep Integration Architecture (4-Phase Roadmap)

### Phase 1: Manual Export/Import (Months 1-3) - MVP
**TAG:** "Export to PitchTracker" button → JSON file
**PitchTracker:** "Import TAG Sports Data" feature
**User:** Manual file transfer
**Timeline:** 2-4 weeks | **Effort:** Low | **Risk:** Low

### Phase 2: Cloud Sync (Months 4-6) - Automatic
**TAG:** Auto-upload practice sessions to cloud
**PitchTracker:** Auto-pull athlete data from cloud
**User:** Zero-touch (OAuth one-time account linking)
**Timeline:** 8-12 weeks | **Effort:** Medium | **Infrastructure:** $100-300/month

### Phase 3: Bluetooth PC Ingest (Months 7-9) - **GAME-CHANGER**
**TAG Device:** Connect directly to facility PC via Bluetooth
**Real-Time:** Velocity streams pitch-by-pitch during facility sessions
**Dual-Mode:** TAG works at home (mobile app) AND at facility (PC integration)
**Cross-Validation:** TAG velocity vs. PitchTracker stereo velocity (builds trust)
**Timeline:** 6-8 weeks | **Requires:** TAG firmware update

**KEY VALUE:** Athletes bring TAG device to facility. Device pairs with PC. Works as secondary velocity source. Parents can watch live in TAG app.

### Phase 4: Bidirectional Insights (Months 10-12) - Value Multiplication
**PitchTracker → TAG:** AI insights flow back to TAG Sports app
**Webhooks:** Real-time notifications (anomalies, coaching notes)
**Live Viewing:** Parents watch facility sessions from TAG app
**Combined Analytics:** ML models using practice + facility data
**Timeline:** 8-12 weeks

## Why Bluetooth PC Ingest is Transformative

**Without Integration:**
- TAG device works at home only (mobile app)
- Athletes need separate devices at facility (TAG + PitchTracker cameras)
- No cross-validation (can't compare measurements)

**With Bluetooth Integration:**
- ✅ TAG device works at home AND facility (dual-mode)
- ✅ Real-time velocity streaming to facility PC
- ✅ Cross-validation (TAG radar vs. PitchTracker stereo = accuracy proof)
- ✅ Parents watch live from TAG app (while kid is at facility)
- ✅ Reduced equipment costs (one TAG device serves both contexts)

**Competitive Moat:** No other consumer device (Pocket Radar, Bushnell) has facility PC integration. This makes TAG Sports the ONLY consumer radar that "levels up" to professional facility training.
