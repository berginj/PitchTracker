## Key Strategic Decisions Made

### Decision 1: Partner with TAG Sports (Don't Compete)
**Rationale:** TAG Sports owns consumer market ($230 portable radar). PitchTracker owns facility market ($1,200 stereo vision). Complementary products create consumer→facility pipeline.
**Impact:** 4-13× revenue increase potential, near-zero CAC, unique competitive positioning

### Decision 2: Lock v1.5.0-pilot as Canonical Build
**Rationale:** Version consistency is trust signal. Pilot partners need stable build reference.
**Impact:** 90-day feature freeze, bug fixes allowed, new features require capability contract approval

### Decision 3: Focus on Facilities, Not Consumers
**Rationale:** Setup complexity prevents consumer adoption. TAG Sports already serves consumers well.
**Impact:** Defer mobile app, portability, social features until facility market validated

### Decision 4: Validate Accuracy Before Scaling
**Rationale:** Unvalidated claims damage trust. Reference comparison required for credibility.
**Impact:** Velocity validation becomes P0 priority, blocks broad marketing until complete

### Decision 5: Capability Contract Governs All New Features
**Rationale:** Feature sprawl without validation fragments product. Need discipline.
**Impact:** 8-point checklist + scoring rubric for every proposed capability

## Risks & Mitigation

### High Risk: TAG Sports Declines Partnership
**Likelihood:** Medium (they may have other priorities or concerns)
**Impact:** Eliminates partnership GTM, reverts to solo strategy
**Mitigation:**
- Strong value proposition ($90K-135K referral revenue potential)
- Low-risk pilot (2-4 weeks dev, 90-day test)
- Warm introduction (research mutual contacts)
- Backup: Solo GTM remains viable (partnership is accelerator, not requirement)

### Medium Risk: Pilot Partners Don't Use Integration
**Likelihood:** Medium (manual export/import has friction)
**Impact:** Partnership value unproven
**Mitigation:**
- Target facilities with high TAG user overlap (10+ athletes)
- Athlete incentives ($50 credit for using integration)
- Move to cloud sync quickly (Phase 2) to eliminate friction

### Medium Risk: Validation Shows Poor Accuracy (>±2.5 mph)
**Likelihood:** Medium (no prior reference comparison)
**Impact:** Credibility damage, pilot partner concerns
**Mitigation:**
- Diagnose root causes (calibration, detection, tracking)
- Narrow operating envelope if necessary
- Be transparent with findings
- Improve algorithms before broader rollout

### Low Risk: Partnership Revenue Below Projections
**Likelihood:** Low-Medium (conservative estimates)
**Impact:** Lower ROI than projected, but still positive
**Mitigation:**
- Pilot validates demand before full commitment
- Adjust referral %, bundles, or pricing based on data
- Solo GTM supplements partnership revenue
